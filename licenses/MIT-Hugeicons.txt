Hugeicons — free stroke-rounded icon collection
================================================

The glyphs embedded in
`core/designsystem/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/orbitai/erp/core/designsystem/icon/OrbitIcons.kt`
are derived from the Hugeicons free collection, published as the npm package
`@hugeicons/core-free-icons` (version 4.3.0) under the MIT licence.

Only the free stroke-rounded style is used. No Hugeicons Pro asset is included in this
repository. The path data was extracted and converted to Compose `ImageVector` form by
`tools/gen_designsystem.py`; the geometry is unmodified.

Upstream: https://github.com/hugeicons/hugeicons-react
Package:  https://www.npmjs.com/package/@hugeicons/core-free-icons
